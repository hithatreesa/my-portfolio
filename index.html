My Journey to DevOps

I graduated with a Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) from Bengaluru North University, where I developed strong fundamentals in computer networks, operating systems, and database architecture.

During my five months as a Junior Web Developer, I loved shipping features, but I became even more fascinated by what happened after the code was written: How do servers get provisioned? How can build errors be caught before manual testing? How can environments be reproduced seamlessly without configuration drift?

That curiosity sparked my shift into DevOps engineering. I am now dedicating my daily focus to hands-on learning: mastering the Linux terminal, writing Bash automation scripts, containerizing services with Docker, and setting up automated CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions.
